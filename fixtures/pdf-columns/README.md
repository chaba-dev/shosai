## Reproduction

`python3 fixtures/pdf-columns/generate.py` reproduces the two first-party
controls. The external PDFs are pinned downloads, not generated test content.
The arXiv excerpt was made with qpdf 12.3.2 from the hash above:

```sh
qpdf --deterministic-id original.pdf --pages . 1-5 -- arxiv-first-five.pdf
```

Thrift's first page is Letter (612 × 792pt): body columns approximately
x54–293 and x317–556. The title's ink is around y24–104; PDFium's loose glyph
boxes start at y20.458 and end at y103.322. Tests deliberately allow those
loose-box ascenders, not a hand-tuned crop at y24 that would cut them off.

The image-only control is intentionally a *synthetic* scan of raster marks,
not a third-party scanned publication and not a test of OCR. Whole-page
fallback preserves that raster even though its marks look like two columns.
