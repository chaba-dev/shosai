## Reproduction

`python3 fixtures/pdf-columns/generate.py` reproduces the four first-party
controls. The external PDFs are pinned downloads, not generated test content.
The arXiv excerpt was made with qpdf 12.3.2 from the hash above:

```sh
qpdf --deterministic-id original.pdf --pages . 1-5 -- arxiv-first-five.pdf
```

Thrift's first page is Letter (612 × 792pt): body columns approximately
x54–293 and x317–556. The title's ink is around y24–104; PDFium's loose glyph
boxes start at y20.458 and end at y103.322. Physical lines use tight painted
glyph bounds, while selection retains loose boxes; region crops add 1pt of
raster padding, limited by neighboring crops' whitespace midpoints. This
exposes real inter-row gaps without cutting off antialiasing at crop edges.

The image-only control is intentionally a *synthetic* scan of raster marks,
not a third-party scanned publication and not a test of OCR. Whole-page
fallback preserves that raster even though its marks look like two columns.

## Exact device-reported pages

CI runs the facts-only ResNet/Attention corpus and the redistributed Thrift
PDF. Thrift pages 2/6/7 contain tiny raster hyphens, not separate figures.
ResNet's plots, network diagrams and tables must own their labels/cells once,
without hiding the surrounding columns. All 8 Thrift and 12 ResNet pages
must split; all 15 Attention pages must remain no-gutter fallbacks.

For full PDFium extraction and actual raster coverage, download originals
locally from `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03385v1` and
`https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762v7`, then run:

```sh
RESNET_PDF=/path/to/resnet.pdf ATTENTION_PDF=/path/to/attention.pdf \
  .agents/dev flutter test test/reader/pdf/papers_corpus_test.dart --reporter expanded
```

The test checks pinned original hashes (ResNet
`1e0651b6810ecba34a3dbc5b5b0209226f889004607c1f203540a48d64e5a93a`, Attention
`bdfaa68d8984f0dc02beaca527b76f207d99b666d31d1da728ee0728182df697`), compares
fresh engine geometry with the facts, and checks every pixel darker than 245
in any RGB channel at 1× for exactly one crop owner. Only intentionally
suppressed running folios are excluded. Set `UPDATE_PAPER_GEOMETRY=1` alongside
those paths to regenerate the facts; update `SHA256SUMS` after reviewing them.
Ordinary tests neither fetch papers nor rewrite fixtures.
