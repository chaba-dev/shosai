Shōsai's first release brings EPUB and PDF reading to desktop, tablet and phone.

### Reading

- Read in pages or continuously, with two-page spreads on larger screens.
- Keep the page quiet: reading controls get out of the way until you need them.
  Turn pages with touch, swipes or the keyboard, with optional page-curl animation.
- Read multi-column PDFs one column at a time with PDF Columns, in screens or
  continuously. Enlarge figures and keep a separate fit choice for each paper.
- Choose Paper, Sepia or Night, adjust EPUB text size and line spacing, and read
  right-to-left and vertical Japanese books with ruby (furigana).
- Keep several books open, resume your place, and peek at references before jumping.

### Notes and search

- Highlight text, bookmark places, and write page notes or notes about a whole book.
- Browse notes across books and search book text and notes from anywhere in the app.

### Your books, your storage

- Import EPUB and PDF files, or open them in Shōsai from other apps.
- Connect desktop folders, Dropbox or Google Drive. Phones and tablets use the
  file picker and cloud connections rather than linked folders.
- Cache source books and choose Keep on this device for offline reading.

### Not in this release

Cross-device sync, CBZ comics, WebDAV and OneDrive are not included. Storage
connections provide access to book files; they do not sync reading positions or notes.
